Petals

Sign In to Save Save View source
  • History
  • Purge
  • Talk (0)

Long Page
This article has lots of content and is very long. It is not advised to read the entirety of this article in one sitting.

Petals are a core game mechanic in florr.io, acting as the player's main tools and weapons.

All players have access to five petal slots by default, and they can gain a maximum of ten petal slots with the Loadout Talent. Players also have an equal number of secondary petal slots, allowing them to switch their petals between their primary and secondary slots. As of 8 October 2026 there are a total of 118 petals in florr.io, with many individual petals having their own defining special characteristic that set them apart from the others.

Overview

When a petal collides with another entity such as a mob, an enemy flower, or an enemy petal, it typically deals Damage. Most petals also receive Damage from collisions, which lowers their Health and subsequently the amount of Damage they deal. When a petal's Health is depleted, it is destroyed temporarily and reappears after the petal's Reload time. Certain petals such as the Antennae do not have Health or Damage attributes at all, meaning they cannot deal or receive Damage. Instead, this variant of petal exists solely to provide some sort of special characteristic to the player, such as the Extra Vision attribute in the case of the Antennae.

When swapping and equipping a new petal, the minimum Reload time is 2.5 seconds. The minimum Reload time can be decreased to zero seconds by upgrading the Reload Talent. This rule only applies when first equipping a petal to replace another, not when reloading the equipped petal. Petals with an original Reload time of 2.5 seconds or more are unaffected by this mechanic.

The 2.0s + 1.0s cooldown time format means that the petal takes 2.0 seconds to Reload, and takes an additional 1.0 seconds after being reloaded to perform its special action, known as the activation delay. For instance, a Rose has an activation delay of 1.5 seconds, and will not apply Healing to the flower until its activation delay passes.

Loadouts

Players can switch their petals by using the number keys. Each slot corresponds to a number based on its location with the number appearing below each slot. Pressing this number switches the active petal and the secondary petal. Petals can also be switched by clicking on either or the petals that the player would like to switch. Pressing R (or X on mobile) completely switches all petals in the active loadout with those in the secondary loadout.

Players can save their current assortment of petals to their desired loadout slots, which can be accessed by pressing K or L plus a number key from 0 to 9 (on PC) or holding X and then tapping on one of the secondary petal slots (on mobile). When switching between loadout slots, the icon of the leftmost petal in each loadout slot is shown.

Obtaining

All petals obtained by the player during game play will have their icons saved in their Inventory and the Crafting Menu. All petals typically range in Rarity, and higher rarity petals can be crafted with lower rarity petals. Some petals are not obtainable at lower rarities, for instance the Antennae starts as the Rare rarity and is unobtainable at Common and Unusual.

Petals are dropped by Mobs once they die and shared in PvE, meaning a player can pick the drops up if they do at least 15% damage to the mob and rank top 4 in terms of damage dealt. For Super+ mobs, the thresholds are 1% and top 25 respectively. Most petals can be purchased from the Shop. Except for in Hel and Rift, it is impossible to steal drops from another player.

Magic variants of certain normal petals, namely the Magic Petals, can also be dropped by mobs when a Mana Orb is equipped. Magic Petals typically have some modified attributes compared to their normal petal counterparts, and they also interact with the Mana system in some way or form.

Trivia

  • Every rarity except Eternal has a petal that starts only at that rarity:
  • Common: Glass
  • Unusual: Missile
  • Rare: Antennae
  • Epic: Mysterious Stick
  • Legendary: Yggdrasil
  • Mythic: Third Eye
  • Ultra: Corruption
  • Super: Blood Sacrifice
  • Unique: Pharaoh's Crown
  • Note that all of the petals above with a lower rarity than listed can not be gained by any legitimate means in the game.
  • A total of 118 petals can be currently found in the game.
    • 30 of those petals are from legacy florr.io, while the other 88 were added ever since florr.io was revamped. However, the 30 legacy petals have some differences from their legacy states:
      • The starting rarity of some petals may not match with their legacy rarity;
      • Certain petals are missing, such as Heavy;
      • The Eternal and Unique rarity is missing;
      • Rare Rose, Epic and Legendary Peas, Heaviest and Fast, Twin and Triplet were renamed to, respectively: Dahlia, Grapes, Heavy and Light;
      • Multiple rarity variants of a certain petal (such as Cactus and its Epic variant) are now in the same lineage of petals (e.g. Cactus);
      • Some petals are dropped by different mobs, and only of a certain rarity or above;
      • Dandelion debuffs flowers by 20% for 15 seconds while stacking, and is not shootable anymore but goes farther away on offensive mode than most other petals, and receives one extra Dandelion at both the Mythic and the Ultra rarity variants;
      • Heavy slows down petal rotation speed;
      • Honey can be dropped to the ground, disappears after some time, attracts mobs to its location while it's on the ground, has a mob rarity limit and mobs can develop immunity to its attraction;
      • Salt reflects 20% damage to flowers, 50% damage to mobs of the same rarity, (n*3) 50% damage to mobs of (n-1) rarity below it (whereas n is the number of the rarities the mob is below the petal, and 1 being the same rarity) and less damage to mobs above its own rarity;
      • Stinger is now 3 petals in 1 at Mythic rarity, and not Legendary, and finally;
      • Yin Yang changes the petal rotation orientation at 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 Yin Yangs in one build, while increasing the petal orbit layers at 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 Yin Yangs in one build (layers start at 1 with zero Yin Yangs, then 2 layers at 2, 3 at 4, 4 at 6, 5 at 8 and all 10 at 10).
    • There are 56 petals starting at the Common rarity, 21 at the Unusual rarity, 10 at the Rare rarity, 2 at the Epic rarity, 2 at the Legendary rarity, 5 at the Mythic rarity, and 5 at the Ultra rarity.
  • Petals, while heavily influencing almost all of them, are directly the subject of 54 Achievements:
    • The "All-seeing" Unusual Achievement, granted to the player once they equip Antennae of any rarity;
    • The 2 "Be gone" Achievements of Legendary and Ultra rarities, granted to the player once they mark, respectively, 1 player and 100 players with Dark Mark;
    • The "Breath in, breath out" Epic secret Achievement, granted to the player once they rapidly equip and unequip Air of any rarity multiple times;
    • The "Collector" Super Achievement, granted to the player once they grab 65 different petal drops in one playthrough, which means without dying;
    • The 4 "Consolation prize" Achievements of the Common, Ultra, Super and Eternal rarities, granted to the player once they lose, respectively, 4 petals of any rarity, 4 Mythic petals, 4 Ultra petals and 4 Super petals from a fail in Crafting;
    • The 7 "Crafter" Achievements of all rarities starting at Unusual, granted to the player once they successfully craft a petal of the rarity corresponding to the Achievement's own from Crafting;
    • The "Digger" Unusual Achievement, granted to the player once they equip Cutter of any rarity;
    • The 3 "Evasive" Achievements of the Rare, Legendary and Ultra rarities, granted to the player once they evade, respectively, 1k, 100k and 10m damage values with Talisman of Evasion;
    • The "Gambler" Legendary Achievement, granted to the player once they insert 1000 Chips of any rarity at Plinko;
    • The 7 "Gear Up" Achievements of all rarities starting at Unusual, granted to the player once they equip, respectively, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 10 petals of the same rarity in one build (the rarity of the petal depends on the Achievement);
    • The "Go to WHERE?" Unusual Achievement, granted to the player once they get marked and successfully sent to Hel by a Mark from another player;
    • The "High roller" Super Achievement, granted to the player once they insert an Ultra or a Super Chip in Plinko
    • The "High volume crafter" Ultra Achievement, granted to the player once they successfully craft, in all of the player's playthroughs, 200k petals of any rarity from Crafting;
    • The "JACKPOT!" Mythic Achievement, granted to the player once they hit the 500x Chip multiplier bucket in Plinko;
    • The "Magician" Epic Achievement, granted to the player once they equip a Mana Orb of any rarity;
    • The "Return to sender" Rare Achievement, granted to the player once they hit a mob with Jelly;
    • The "Rolling in dough" Legendary Achievement, granted to the player once they grab 100 petal drops from different mobs in one second or less;
    • The "Sacrifice" Mythic Achievement, granted to the player once they die one second (or less) after the player revives someone with a Yggdrasil;
    • The "Saved" Legendary Achievement, granted to the player once a player revives the player with Yggdrasil;
    • The 2 "Savior" Achievements of Legendary and Ultra rarities, granted to the player once they revive, respectively, 1 player and 100 players with Yggdrasil;
    • The 2 "Sound barrier" Achievements of Mythic and Ultra rarities, granted to the player once they move a lot of tiles in a very short amount of time, usually achieved with Bubbles or Rubbers;
    • The 4 "Summoner" Achievements of the Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic rarities, granted to the player once they spawn, respectively, 1, 100, 1k and 10k friendly mobs;
    • The "Termites…?" Rare Achievement, granted to the player once they spawn a friendly Soldier Termite from using both Ant Egg and Relic in the same build;
    • The "The all-seeing one" and "It was destiny" Achievements of Legendary and Ultra rarities, which, despite having different names, share almost the same completion method as if they had the same name: they are granted to the player once they, respectively, craft a petal of any rarity and an Ultra Petal with Oracle;
    • The "The lucky one" Legendary Achievement, granted to the player once they craft 10 petals in a row without fail from Crafting;
    • The "The unlucky one" Ultra Achievement, granted to the player once they fail 100 petal crafts in a row without success from Crafting;
    • The 3 "Trade" Achievements of Epic, Mythic and Super rarities, granted to the player once they trade, respectively, a petal of any rarity, a Mythic petal and a Super petal for Coin from Trader, and finally;
    • The "Wasteful Crafting" Mythic Achievement, granted to the player once they lose 1m petals from fails in Crafting, in all of the player's playthroughs.
